How it works

From checkout to evidence in three steps.

01

Agent signs the request

The AI shopping agent attaches an HTTP Signature and a mandate to every checkout request, binding the order to the shopper's authorisation.

02

ProveTheBuy verifies it

Your store calls the verifier. It checks the signature against the agent's directory key, validates the mandate, detects replays, and applies your policy in <5 ms.

03

Dispute? Evidence is ready.

The verification record, key snapshot, and order hash are stored in a tamper-evident ledger. When a chargeback arrives, the evidence pack is one click away.
